Founder and Director of multiple research centers and collaborative research initiatives; Principal Investigator of major research projects (2006–2023).

Editor of Russian translations of key texts in the Durkheimian tradition and cultural sociology, including the first Russian translation of The Elementary Forms of Religious Life.

Associate Editor, American Journal of Cultural Sociology (since 2019).

Member of editorial boards including Russian Sociological Review and Sociální studia / Social Studies. Member of the American Sociological Association (Theory and Culture Sections) and the International Sociological Association; Editor of Theory, newsletter of ISA RC16 Sociological Theory (2018–2022).
